## [HOW IT WORKS — 3ステップ]

**Section title:** From "I should do sales" to "it's on my calendar" — in one message.

1. **Ask Claude.**
   *"Book 3 meetings next week with Series A fintech founders in NYC. Budget $450."*

2. **Humans do the outreach.**
   Vetted freelance SDRs run the outbound. AI handles matching, qualification, and quality control behind the scenes.

3. **You show up.**
   BANT-qualified meetings land on your calendar with a full briefing on each prospect. That's it.

---

## [WHY IT'S DIFFERENT — 差別化]

**Section title:** Not a lead list. Not another tool to manage.

- **You get meetings, not leads.** No lists to chase, no sequences to write. The deliverable is a confirmed meeting.
- **Pay per result.** You're billed per qualified meeting that actually books — not per seat, not per month.
- **Zero overhead.** No SDR to hire, train, or manage. No CRM to wire up. You never talk to us.

---

## [WHO IT'S FOR]

**Section title:** Built for founders who'd rather build than sell.

- Technical founders who hate (or freeze on) outbound
- Solo consultants who need pipeline but not a sales hire
- Busy CEOs whose calendar is the bottleneck
